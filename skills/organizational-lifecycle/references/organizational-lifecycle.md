# Organizational life cycle stages

Source: https://gist.github.com/kbaribeau/2e445d09e8377cd812f5af7bf356dec4
Framework reference: https://www.adizes.com/organizational-lifecycle

## When to use

When starting work with a new company or client, use these notes to understand its lifecycle stage and consider how to help it advance.
Keep company-specific assessments outside this repository.

## Original gist

The source text is preserved below, including its informal wording and numerical claims.
Those claims and attributed observations have not been independently verified; treat them as source notes rather than established statistics.

```text
Organizational life cycle stages -- https://www.adizes.com/organizational-lifecycle
  * courtship
    org is not born
    excitement between founders (parents)
    parents are usually overly optimistic, can make promises they don't keep
    if risk is accepted, the organization is born

    founders tend to be people who are RISK TOLERANT
      this can become dangerous at later stages of growth

  * infant

    entrepreneur needs now to produce
    infant requires food (cash)
    very vulnerable on founders (for everything)
    little space/crowded
    management by crisis, inconsistency, no planning

    FIRE FIGHTING is necessary to survive infancy

    99% of infant companies don't survive infancy
      can't produce sales or cash

      An advisor reports that every founder they have seen in 10 years has been dominant in red (focus on completing tasks) and blue (knowledge building). These are needed to get passed the infant and toddler stages.

  * toddler
    * vision starts to become clear
    * progress reported in terms of MAINLY sales growth (not profits)
    * Abundance of opportunities (few priorities)
    * hire tomorrow what was needed yesterday
    * often runs out of cash
    * lack of delegation among founders
    * "hub and spoke" is common and comfortable

    what percent of the 1% of companies that survived infancy make it into adolesence?
      4%

  * adolescent
    * shift from entrepreneurialism to professional management
    * founder accepts policy and the org is born again separate from the founder
    * conflicts between administrators and entrepreneurs over risk and growth expectations
    * goals are established but interpretation often does not cascade through the org
    * typical to sacrifice the future for current profits
    * lanes among leaders typically formed
    * leaders leverage their differentiable talents, delegate the ordinary
    * new organization of accountability causes some to opt out

    need STRUCTURE and PROCESS
      Note: "what got me here will get me there" KILLS companies at this stage
      RISK TOLERANCE needs to be fought

  * Adult
    results oritented
    follow systems
    creates an follows plans
    concerned with PREDICTABLE sales AND profits
    begines to create NEW INFANTS, often without qualified managers

    PEOPLE MANAGEMENT shows up here
      makes sure the 200th an 10000th person who joins the company is inspired
      and feels appreciated

      make sure that we can maintain stability through turnover
```
